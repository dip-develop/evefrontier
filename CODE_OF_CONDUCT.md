# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of this project pledge to make participation in EVE Frontier an inclusive, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Showing empathy towards others
- Helping newcomers and encouraging growth

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or intimidation in any form
- Disrespectful or derogatory comments and personal attacks
- Publishing private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions or contributions

## Our Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — including GitHub issues, pull requests, and community forums — and also applies when individuals are representing the project publicly.
